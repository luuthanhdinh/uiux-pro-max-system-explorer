## Design System: AI SaaS Platform

Generated from ui-ux-pro-max skill · Product: AI / SaaS

### Pattern
- **Name:** Hero + Features + Pricing + Dashboard
- **Sections:** 1. Landing (AI demo hero), 2. Pricing tiers, 3. AI Workspace (dashboard)
- **CTA Placement:** Hero sticky + Post-features + Pricing cards
- **Conversion Focus:** Show AI value immediately (live demo in hero). Pricing after trust established.

### Style
- **Primary:** AI-Native UI — gradient mesh backgrounds, subtle glow on AI elements, streaming text animations
- **Secondary:** Minimalism — generous white space, clean grid, no decorative elements
- **Key Effects:** Typing cursor animation, shimmer on AI response, 200ms hover transitions

### Typography
- **Heading:** Poppins (400/500/600/700) — modern, approachable, startup energy
- **Body:** Open Sans (300/400/500/600) — highly legible at 14-16px
- **Google Fonts:** `https://fonts.google.com/share?selection.family=Poppins:wght@400;500;600;700|Open+Sans:wght@300;400;500;600`

### Anti-patterns
- Don't use dark mode by default (breaks trust for business/enterprise buyers)
- Avoid overusing purple — reserve for primary actions only
- No infinite scroll in pricing — users need to compare plans easily

### Pages

| Pricing | Conversion | 3-tier cards, feature comparison, FAQ |
| Dashboard | Retention | Chat interface, model selector, prompt history |
